Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is delivery across Kenya?

Nairobi orders are delivered same-day or within 24 hours. Upcountry deliveries (Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, Nakuru) take 1-2 business days via Dokani Courier / Fargo.

How do I pay with M-Pesa?

During checkout, select M-Pesa. You can use our Buy Goods Till Number 5829104 or request an STK push directly to your phone.

What is the return & exchange policy?

We provide a 7-day hassle-free exchange or return guarantee on unworn footwear and undamaged goods with original tags intact.
